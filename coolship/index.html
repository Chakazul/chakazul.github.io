Coolship steer by cognitive masking

A spaceship-steering toy played inside Lenia, a continuous cellular automaton. The ship is steered by cognitive masking (Cool et al. 2026): a masked area is hidden from the creature's own senses, dropping out of every kernel that reads that color channel. Interventions (masking, erasing, or inserting) to a part of the ship have various effects: the ship may change direction or speed, change into another morph, or die out.
In the ship view (right), press and hold on the zoomed-in creature to intervene that area, and observe the effects. The top patches choose the channel (1 2 3); the bottom ones choose the action: mask, erase or insert mass (4 5 6). In the world view (left) click one ship to follow it in the ship view.
