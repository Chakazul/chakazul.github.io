Cognoship Lab steer by cognitive masking

Chart History

A spaceship-steering toy played inside Lenia, a continuous cellular automaton. The ship is steered by cognitive masking (Cool et al. 2026): a masked area is hidden from the creature's own senses, dropping out of every kernel that reads that color channel. Interventions (masking, erasing, or drawing) to a part of the ship have various effects: the ship may change direction or speed, change into another morph, or die out.
In the ship view (right), press and hold on the zoomed-in creature to intervene that area, and observe the effects. The top patches choose the channel (1 2 3); the bottom ones choose the action: mask, erase or draw mass (4 5 6). In the world view (left) click one ship to follow it in the ship view. Detect names the morph of the followed ship (Focused) or of every ship (Every, the default), by comparing its recent stats with the recorded ones of each morph; ??? when it is not sure.
