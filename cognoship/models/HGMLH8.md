Hold in steps (30 steps = 1 s at speed 1). `p` = share of the trials (start phases and angles) that gave this outcome. Turns are measured once it is named again, against an untouched copy from the same start. A mirror-image ship (turning the other way) takes the mirrored action: left and right swapped, turn negated.
