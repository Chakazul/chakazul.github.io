# KZ2BLD morph model

Generated by `tools/models.py book` from headless experiments (see the tool's docstring). R = 18; mask disc radius 4 cells (the game's). 
Offsets `(fwd, side)` are in R from the centre of mass: + fwd = ahead, + side = to the right as seen. Turns in degrees, + = clockwise (right); every morph is started turning + (mirrored if its pattern turns −). Hold in steps (30 steps = 1 s at speed 1). `p` = share of the trials (start phases and angles) that gave this outcome. Actions: mask the channel at a spot for a hold; spots are the centre, or a direction in the ship's frame (front, front-right, ...) on the body (0.45 R from the centre) or at its edge (0.9 R). *Name box*: what the game's detection shows, start to end (??? = not named: not steerable by this book). Turns are measured once it is named again, against an untouched copy from the same start. A mirror-image ship (turning the other way) takes the mirrored action: left and right swapped, turn negated.
