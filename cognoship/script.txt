You are traveling in a spaceship.
Alone.
Near the _Proxima Centauri_ exoplanet system, 4 light-years from Earth.
With recursive self-improvement technology, we can travel far, far away.

We can't control the ship with buttons or levers, though.
The ship is made from _Lenium_, a new type of agentic material.
Thankfully, _Lenium_ actively senses and reacts to its surroundings.
So we can steer the ship by blocking parts of its *cognitive field*.
In the panel, try clicking on the ship to mask its senses.[need:first_touch]
Nice! You can now steer the ship, and even change its shape.
At the top, you can select which color {R}{G}{B}{:1}{:2}{:3} to mask.
